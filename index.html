Your turn

One prompt.
A place to begin.

Pick a prompt, replace the details in brackets, and paste it into your AI chat. Keep what helps. Make the result your own.

Open ChatGPT ↗Open Claude ↗

Turn one idea into a useful content plan.

I want to help [audience] understand [topic]. My main message is [idea], and I have [time available] to create this week.

Ask me up to three questions to fill any important gaps. Then suggest three distinct posts: one quick tip, one demonstration, and one answer to a common question.

For each, give me a clear opening line, three talking points, the simplest visual I could make, and a next step for the viewer. Use plain language and only facts I can verify. Keep the plan realistic for my available time.

Make a short video easier to create.

Help me turn [idea] into a 30-second tutorial for [audience]. The one thing a viewer should learn is [takeaway].

Create a scene-by-scene table with timing, a natural voiceover, the visual to show, and short on-screen text. Keep the narration under 70 words. Suggest visuals I can film or create with AI, and clearly label any AI-generated scene ideas.

Use only the product details I provide. If a step or result needs checking, flag it instead of inventing it. End with a short production checklist and one clear next step for the viewer.

Make a messy list feel manageable.

Here is what is on my mind today: [tasks and deadlines]. I have [available hours], and my energy level is [low / medium / high].

Help me sort the list into must-do, can-wait, and needs-a-decision. Ask about any unclear deadline rather than assuming it.

Suggest a realistic order with time estimates and short breaks. Break the hardest task into a first step I can finish in 10 minutes. If the list will not fit, show me what to move. End with a three-item checklist for today.

Make the details yours before you use it.

A little about me

It started with
curiosity.

I’m Matilda Nkem, the person behind Digital Tilda. My journey with AI began in 2019 with a curiosity that hasn’t left me: how does this technology work, and what could it change about the way we live, work, and create?

That curiosity grew into a deeper commitment to learning. In 2022, I completed my MBA with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence. I’m proud of that milestone, and I’m just as excited by what there is still to discover.

Today, I share that exploration through Digital Tilda. I turn what I learn into practical guides, useful prompts, and approachable tutorials, whether you’re planning content, creating your first AI image, building a page, or finding an easier way to get something done.

You don’t need to have it all figured out to start. Bring an idea, a question, or a little curiosity. Let’s see what you can create.
