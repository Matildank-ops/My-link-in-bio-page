A note before you begin

200 Creative Prompts for Product Visuals

The video promises “200 more codes” for images, ads and video. This guide delivers 200 adaptable prompts: twenty creative jobs across ten realistic product contexts. Words like “/headshot” and “/product spin” in the clip are shorthand, not guaranteed ChatGPT commands. Some formats require a separate video product or an installed third-party plugin.

Use only images, likenesses and product claims you have permission to use. For product ads, compare generated packaging and labels to the real item before publishing.

Chapter 011 min read

Choose the correct tool and brief

ChatGPT Images can create and edit still images from text or an uploaded reference. A storyboard, shot list or animation brief can be drafted in ChatGPT; producing a video requires a video-capable tool and its own account access. If you install a plugin, confirm its current actions and permissions in the directory.

A strong brief states the subject, audience, placement, visual style, exact facts that must remain true, and what should not be invented. For a headshot, include an authorized reference and ask to preserve identity. For product imagery, include real packaging and a color reference.

✦ Ready to use

Create three distinct creative directions for [product] aimed at [audience]. For each, give the key image, claim to verify, placement, aspect ratio and one reason the direction might fail.

Review before publishing

Check identity, hands, labels, logos, geometry, price and claims. Keep an original product photo or approved brand asset beside the output for comparison. For social media, preview the actual mobile crop. For ads, verify platform rules and any claim substantiation before release.

Fast quality pass: Is the subject recognizable? Is the promise true? Can a viewer understand the point in two seconds? Is the text legible at final size? If any answer is no, revise the brief and regenerate only the weak part.
